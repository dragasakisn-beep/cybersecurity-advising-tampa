Student Advising SMS
Operator: Prof. Dragassakis KU Cybersecurity Program Advisor.

SMS Terms

The proposed service sends advising check-ins, reminders, appointment coordination, and relevant responses. Expect approximately one weekly check-in, plus follow-ups. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. No purchase or payment to the operator is required to opt in.

Voluntary consent

Messages begin only after documented explicit consent to this service. Participation is optional and does not change enrollment or access to advising. Viewing these pages does not enroll anyone. A phone number provided for enrollment does not by itself enroll a student in this messaging program.

Stopping and restarting

Reply STOP to opt out. A final confirmation may follow, after which further advising texts stop unless permission is renewed. START, YES, or UNSTOP may be used to restore permission once those keywords are configured for this service. Keyword handling must be tested before live messaging begins.

Help and contact

Reply HELP for help or contact the operator through the support email that must be confirmed before launch. The final HELP response will identify the service and a working support contact.

AI assistance and adviser review

AI may assist in drafting responses and summarizing messages. Initially, the adviser reviews messages before sending and reviews notes before posting to Anthology. Messages are not guarantees of enrollment, financial assistance, academic exceptions, or other institutional decisions. Confirm consequential advice through the appropriate official university channel.

Availability

This service is not monitored continuously and is not an emergency service. Do not rely on it for urgent assistance. Delivery can be delayed or fail; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy and records

Read the Privacy Policy for the proposed information handling and consent practices. Avoid sensitive information by SMS. The service must obtain the necessary authorization before processing student records or adding notes to institutional systems.

Current status

The service is in development. These draft terms do not claim university endorsement, carrier approval, completed consent collection, or working automated messaging. Draft prepared November 10, 2026. They must be finalized to match the deployed service before use.
