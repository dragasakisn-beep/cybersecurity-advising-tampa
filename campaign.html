These materials document the proposed Student Advising SMS campaign. They are drafts, not evidence of collected consent or provider approval.

Sender and registration

Prof. Dragassakis KU Cybersecurity Program Advisor is the supplied operator identity. The work concerns university advising. An approved individual brand does not resolve eligibility for this activity; provider clarification is pending. These documents do not reclassify the activity as an independent business.

Campaign description

Two-way, non-marketing advising check-ins, reminders, appointment coordination, and relevant follow-ups for participating students. Planned volume: approximately 240 outgoing messages and 240 replies in a four-week month. AI assists with drafts, initially reviewed by the adviser.

Verbal consent script

This is Prof. Dragassakis, your KU adviser. May Student Advising SMS text you at this number with weekly check-ins, reminders, and follow-ups? Messages may be automated or AI-assisted. Frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is optional and does not affect enrollment or advising. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Avoid sensitive details. Please review this service's Privacy Policy and SMS Terms. Do you agree?

Before using this script, provide students the final published policy and terms links. The operator must record the affirmative response, number, date, adviser, and script version. Do not publish actual consent records or signed student documents.

Sample messages

Check-in: Hi [Student Name], this is Prof. Dragassakis, your KU adviser, with Student Advising SMS. How are classes going this week? Is there anything you would like help with? Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Follow-up: Hi [Student Name], this is Prof. Dragassakis with Student Advising SMS. Thank you for your update. Would you like to discuss next steps? Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Keyword and reply plan

Opt-in: START, YES, UNSTOP. Opt-out: STOP and provider-supported equivalents. Help: HELP. These behaviors must be configured and tested before launch; listing them here does not activate them.

Proposed opt-in confirmation: Student Advising SMS: You are opted in to advising texts from Prof. Dragassakis. Approximately one weekly check-in plus follow-ups; frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Proposed opt-out confirmation: Student Advising SMS: You have opted out and will receive no further advising texts unless you opt in again.

Campaign terms

The Terms & Conditions apply to the proposed SMS service. The Privacy Policy describes proposed data handling. No live service is offered, and support contact, retention, security configuration, and registration must be resolved before enrollment.
