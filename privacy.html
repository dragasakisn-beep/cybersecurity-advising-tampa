Privacy Policy

Service in preparation. These documents are drafts. Support contact, data handling, and registration details must be finalized before SMS enrollment or campaign submission.

Student Advising SMS is a planned personally operated advising service. Operator: Prof. Dragassakis KU Cybersecurity Program Advisor.

Scope

This draft covers the proposed messaging service and this informational site. It does not replace university privacy policies or establish permission to access or disclose university records. Student enrollment alone is not treated as proof of consent to this service.

Information used for messaging

The planned service will use the participant's name, mobile number, consent and opt-out records, messages, delivery information, and adviser follow-up notes. Only information needed for advising, service operation, and consent management should be collected. Do not send sensitive identifiers, financial account details, or medical records by SMS.

Purpose

Information will be used to deliver requested advising texts, respond to questions, prepare adviser-reviewed replies and notes, maintain consent preferences, troubleshoot delivery, and protect the service. Any transfer of notes to a university system requires authorized access and an appropriate basis for that transfer.

SMS consent and marketing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and messaging consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with affiliates or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes. Consent applies only to this service and is not transferred to other senders or campaigns.

Service providers and AI

Twilio is the proposed SMS delivery provider. If activated, approved hosting, storage, and AI providers may process the minimum information necessary to operate the service. AI may assist in preparing replies and summaries; the initial workflow requires adviser review before sending or posting. The final provider list, processing terms, and retention settings must be confirmed before any real student data is processed. This draft makes no claim that data is never retained or never used for training by every provider.

Retention and security

The operator must establish a retention and deletion schedule for messages, consent evidence, delivery records, and backups before launch. Access controls, secure credential storage, and encryption appropriate to each system must also be configured before real data is used. No specific retention period or implemented security certification is asserted by this draft. Ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted.

Choices and requests

Participation is voluntary. Reply STOP to stop further advising messages; a final opt-out confirmation may be sent. Reply HELP for assistance. Ask the operator about access, correction, deletion, or another advising channel. Some consent or suppression records may need to be retained to honor opt-outs or meet applicable obligations. The support email must be supplied before launch.

This website

These static pages have no forms, advertising trackers, external fonts, or analytics added by the operator. GitHub Pages, if used to host them, may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under GitHub's own privacy practices. Never publish student records in this site's repository.

Updates

Material changes to the actual service must be reflected in this policy and communicated to participants where appropriate. Draft prepared November 10, 2026; not an effective-date declaration for a live service.
